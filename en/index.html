FAQ

Is the 0.4% a freight charge?

No. The 0.4% is not freight. It applies only on the Wuqia / Kyrgyzstan route and is 0.4% of the goods' invoice value. Freight is quoted separately. Khorgos does not add it.

22 days to where?

About 22 days to the Tehran terminal, not to your door. Mashhad and Sarakhs are not given a day count and are quote only.
