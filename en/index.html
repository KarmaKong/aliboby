Step-by-Step Overland Transit: China Consolidation Hubs to Tehran Customs

  1. Day 01–02

    Origin hub consolidation

    Consolidation at Yiwu Warehouse (Zhejiang) and Shenzhen Pinghu Distribution Center (Guangdong). Container stuffing, export declaration filing, and TIR manifest pre-recording.

  2. Day 03–04

    Khorgos border processing

    Arrival at Khorgos Free Trade Zone. China Customs export clearance and physical sealing of mega curtainsider trailers under customs supervision with an international TIR Carnet endorsement.

  3. Day 05–11

    Eurasian land corridor transit

    Non-stop convoy movement across Kazakhstan (Almaty–Shymkent) and Turkmenistan (Farap–Mary) under TIR customs seal. No transit unsealing, no offloading, no escrow bond delays.

  4. Day 12–14

    Sarakhs customs border crossing

    Entry through Sarakhs border crossing into Iran. TIR seal verification, entry into Iran's ASYCUDA customs transit database, and bonded dispatch under transit escort.

  5. Day 15–18

    Arrival at Tehran West Customs

    Convoy delivers into the Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb) bonded warehouse. A warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar) is issued to the consignee's licensed clearance broker for duty payment and release.

FAQ

Does chinairantrucks provide DDP door-to-door delivery inside Iran?

No. We specialize strictly in cross-border linehaul road freight (CPT / DAP terms) from China hubs directly to Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), Aprin Dry Port, or Shahriyar Customs. On arrival the cargo enters the customs bonded warehouse and your licensed broker handles import duty payment and local collection.

What is the exact transit time to Tehran West Customs?

14 to 18 calendar days from warehouse departure (Yiwu or Shenzhen) to warehouse entry at Tehran West Customs, including exit clearance at Khorgos and inbound processing at Sarakhs.

Can you transport UN 3480 standalone lithium-ion batteries by road?

Yes. Road freight under the TIR and ADR conventions permits commercial movement of UN 3480 / 3481 Class 9 lithium batteries, energy storage systems, and industrial raw materials with a valid MSDS and UN packaging documentation.

What is the minimum volume for LCL consolidation?

Our weekly scheduled consolidation accepts cargo from 1 CBM or 100 kg at the Yiwu and Shenzhen facilities, terminating at Tehran West Customs.
