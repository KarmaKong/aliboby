TRANSIT

Central Asia transit

From Xinjiang to Iran, the truck has to cross Central Asia. The two corridors differ in which countries they transit. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal, not the door.

  • Wuqia route transits: Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan (adds 0.4%)
  • Khorgos route transits: Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan (no 0.4%)
  • Both cross Turkmenistan; transit control is strict, convoy scheduling varies
  • The main routes do not run through Tajikistan

The Wuqia route

Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. Crossing Kyrgyzstan adds the 0.4% invoice-value tax. See Wuqia, Irkeshtam, and the 0.4% (Persian).

The Khorgos route

Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. No 0.4%.

The Turkmenistan leg

Both corridors cross Turkmenistan. Transit control there is strict; convoy scheduling follows conditions on the day.

TIR

Sealed at origin, fewer openings and truck changes en route. Fewer, not zero. TIR does not fix a day count or the freight. See TIR.

Tajikistan

The main routes do not run through Tajikistan; specific cases are quoted separately.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
