TRANSIT

Central Asia transit: Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan under TIR

Between Khorgos and Iran the truck crosses two countries: Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan. It runs under a TIR carnet, so there is no transit duty, no per-border deposit, no unsealing and no transloading.

  • Main corridor: Kazakhstan (Almaty–Shymkent), then Turkmenistan (Farap–Mary), then Sarakhs
  • Under TIR: no transit duty, no escrow bond, sealed once at Khorgos, opened at Tehran Customs
  • Two drivers rotate; the two-country leg is roughly 7 days
  • The Irkeshtam variant runs Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan instead and adds the 0.4% invoice-value tax
  • The main routes do not run through Tajikistan

The Kazakhstan leg

From Khorgos through Almaty and Shymkent toward the Turkmenistan border. Roughly 3–4 days. Winter road conditions are the main variable.

The Turkmenistan leg

From Farap to Mary and on to the Iranian border. Transit control is strict and convoy scheduling follows conditions on the day. Then the truck reaches Sarakhs.

Why TIR matters here

The carnet is what removes the deposit at each border and the container stripping. The load is sealed once, at Khorgos, and opened once, at the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse. See how TIR works.

The Irkeshtam variant

For capacity or routing, the truck can instead transit Kyrgyzstan and then Uzbekistan before Turkmenistan. That routing adds the 0.4% invoice-value transit tax.

Tajikistan

The main routes do not pass through Tajikistan. Specific cases are quoted separately.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Which countries does the truck cross?

On the main corridor: Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan. On the Irkeshtam variant: Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan.

Is the cargo opened in transit?

No. Under the TIR carnet the load is sealed at Khorgos and stays sealed until the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse.

Does it go through Tajikistan?

No. The main routes do not run through Tajikistan.

What does TIR remove on this leg?

The per-border security deposit and the physical stripping of the load at each transit country.
