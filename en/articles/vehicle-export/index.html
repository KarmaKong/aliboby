Vehicle exports to Central Asia and Iran

2026-09-04

Khorgos exported about 450,000 vehicles in 2025, up 6.9% year on year and a record (Sina Finance, 2026-01). Xinjiang exported 178,000 finished vehicles for the year, 80% via Khorgos, led by new-energy vehicles. Complete-vehicle export has a different bar and a different method from general cargo.

  • Khorgos exported about 450,000 vehicles in 2025 (+6.9%), China's largest road port for vehicle exports
  • Complete-vehicle export needs China CCC, a Certificate of Conformity (CoC), and vehicle papers; some models / destinations need an export licence
  • Complete vehicles mostly move on car-carrier block trains plus frame racks / ro-ro, not curtainsiders or LCL
  • Our line carries general cargo hub-to-customs in 14–18 days to Tehran West Customs; complete vehicles, EVs and heavy machinery are quoted separately

Crossings: Khorgos leads for vehicles

Xinjiang exported 178,000 finished vehicles in 2025, 80% via Khorgos; Irkeshtam handles a small volume. Destinations are mainly Central Asia and Russia. Vehicles for Iran run the same Central Asian corridor and then into Iran. Khorgos is a rail-and-road port, and vehicle export is mostly by block train.

Documents: a few more than general cargo

China CCC (compulsory product certification): generally required to export a complete vehicle. Certificate of Conformity (CoC): the destination often requires it for registration and clearance. Export licence / quota: applies to some models and destinations. Vehicle papers: certificate, invoice, packing list, VIN list. A new-energy vehicle carries a traction battery and is declared as dangerous / special cargo, not as a plain vehicle.

Method: block train plus racks / ro-ro, not a curtainsider

The mainstream for vehicle export is a car-carrier (JSQ) block train, frame racks, or ro-ro, a batch at a time. That is a different service from the curtainsider FTL / LCL we run: loading, pricing, documents and transit time all differ. Squeezing a few vehicles into a curtainsider is uneconomic and raises securing and declaration issues.

Common mistakes

“Complete vehicles are easier to ship than general cargo” — the opposite: a higher bar, more documents, tighter scheduling. “A new-energy vehicle is just a normal vehicle” — it carries a battery and is declared as special cargo. “Reaching Central Asia is the same as reaching Iran” — Central Asia is transit; vehicles for Iran still need a broker and a handover at the destination customs. “14–18 days” — that is our curtainsider hub-to-customs figure to Tehran West Customs; a vehicle block train is quoted separately.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Do you carry complete vehicles?

Our line carries general cargo (auto parts, electronics, machinery, building materials) hub-to-customs in 14–18 days. Complete vehicles, EVs and heavy machinery are quoted separately as a project.

What documents does vehicle export need?

Generally China CCC, the destination's Certificate of Conformity, the vehicle certificate and a VIN list; some models / destinations also need an export licence.

Can a new-energy vehicle be declared as a normal vehicle?

No. It carries a traction battery and is declared as dangerous / special cargo.
