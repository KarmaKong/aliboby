PORTS

Xinjiang exit crossings

From Xinjiang to Iran, trucks use two kinds of crossing: toward Kyrgyzstan, or toward Kazakhstan. Both are about 22 days to the Tehran terminal.

  • Toward Kyrgyzstan: Irkeshtam (Wuqia), Torugart — adds the 0.4% invoice-value tax
  • Toward Kazakhstan: Khorgos, Alashankou — no 0.4%
  • Alashankou is mainly rail; Iran cargo mostly runs Irkeshtam and Khorgos
  • The China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan railway is under construction, not open

Toward Kyrgyzstan

Irkeshtam (the crossing beyond Wuqia) is the main one, with Torugart as an alternative. Chain: Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. Crossing Kyrgyzstan adds the 0.4% invoice-value tax.

Toward Kazakhstan

Khorgos is the main one, with Alashankou as an alternative. Chain: Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. No 0.4%.

Road and rail

Alashankou is mainly a rail port. The China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan railway is under construction and not open; Iran cargo is still road. See the CKU railway (Chinese).

The 0.4% is only on the Kyrgyzstan side. See Wuqia or Khorgos.
