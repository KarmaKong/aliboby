WUQIA

Wuqia or Khorgos

Two exits from Xinjiang. The difference that matters to your cost is the 0.4%.

  • Wuqia (via Kyrgyzstan): freight + 0.4% of the goods' invoice value
  • Khorgos (via Kazakhstan): freight, no 0.4%
  • The 0.4% is a tax, not freight — two separate line items
  • Both are about 22 days to the Tehran terminal

Wuqia · Irkeshtam

Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. Crossing Kyrgyzstan adds 0.4% of the goods' invoice value. Freight and that 0.4% are billed separately.

Khorgos

Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. No 0.4%.

Which is cheaper overall depends on the rate on the day and your invoice value. We do not publish rates.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
