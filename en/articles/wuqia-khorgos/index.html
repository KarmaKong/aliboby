KHORGOS

Khorgos or Irkeshtam: the 0.4% is only on the Irkeshtam route

The main corridor leaves China at Khorgos into Kazakhstan. Irkeshtam, via Kyrgyzstan, is the secondary routing, and it carries a 0.4% invoice-value transit tax. Khorgos does not.

  • Khorgos (into Kazakhstan) — primary routing, no 0.4%
  • Irkeshtam (into Kyrgyzstan, then Uzbekistan) — secondary routing, adds 0.4% of invoice value
  • The 0.4% is a tax on the goods' invoice value, billed separately — it is not freight
  • Both routings reach the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse in 14–18 days

Why Khorgos is the default

The Kazakhstan corridor (Almaty–Shymkent, then Turkmenistan) carries no per-shipment transit tax, and our scheduled convoys run it every Tuesday and Friday. Unless there is a capacity or routing reason, your cargo goes via Khorgos.

When Irkeshtam is used

Occasionally, for capacity or routing. The truck then transits Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan, and the 0.4% invoice-value tax applies, shown as a separate line on the invoice.

The 0.4% is not freight

It is 0.4% of the goods' invoice value, a transit tax on the Kyrgyzstan leg. Freight is quoted separately and does not change because of it.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Is the 0.4% a freight charge?

No. It is a tax equal to 0.4% of the goods' invoice value, applied only when the routing crosses Kyrgyzstan (Irkeshtam). It is billed as a separate line.

Which route will my cargo take?

Khorgos into Kazakhstan by default. Irkeshtam is used only for capacity or routing reasons, and we tell you if it applies.

Does Khorgos add any tax?

No. The Khorgos–Kazakhstan corridor has no per-shipment transit tax.

Is one route faster?

No meaningful difference. Both are planned at 14–18 days to Tehran West Customs.
