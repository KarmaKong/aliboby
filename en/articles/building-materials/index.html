Shipping building materials by truck from China to Iran

Building materials from China to Iran by road. Out of Xinjiang, two corridors, about 22 days to the Tehran terminal. Not to the door. Not by sea.

  • Mostly dense cargo: charged by weight, usually full truckload
  • Give gross weight and piece count
  • Wuqia adds 0.4% of invoice value; Khorgos does not
  • About 22 days to the Tehran terminal, not the door

Wuqia

Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. Freight quoted on request. Crossing Kyrgyzstan adds a 0.4% invoice-value tax. Freight and that 0.4% are two separate items.

Khorgos

Xinjiang → Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran. Freight quoted on request. No 0.4%.

Both are about 22 days to the Tehran terminal. Destination cities: Mashhad, Tehran, Sarakhs; the latter two are not given a day count.

How to describe the goods

Tiles, sanitaryware, hardware, pipe, board — state the category, not "a batch of building materials". Mostly dense cargo: charged by weight, usually full truckload. Give gross weight and piece count.

The 0.4% is only on the Wuqia route. Khorgos does not add it. This page does not compare which is cheaper. Wuqia or Khorgos
