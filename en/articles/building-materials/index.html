Shipping building materials by truck from China to Iran

building materials from China to Iran by road. Consolidated at Yiwu, Shenzhen and Guangzhou, out through Khorgos on a sealed TIR trailer, delivered in 14–18 days to the bonded customs warehouse at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb). Hub-to-customs, not to the door, not by sea.

  • 14–18 calendar days, origin hub to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse
  • Hub-to-customs · CPT / DAP · the consignee’s broker clears and pays duty
  • Route: Khorgos — Kazakhstan — Turkmenistan — Sarakhs — Tehran
  • FTL and LCL from 1 CBM or 100 kg, weekly Tuesday & Friday departures

The route

Origin hub → export clearance and sealing at Khorgos (TIR lock + electronic seal) → transit through Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan (no unsealing, no transloading) → entry at Sarakhs → the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse, where a warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar) is issued to your broker. The Irkeshtam crossing (via Kyrgyzstan) is the secondary routing and carries a 0.4% invoice-value transit tax; Khorgos does not.

Describing the goods and loading

Stone, tiles, sanitaryware, profiles, hardware. Mostly heavy cargo, charged by weight; the load plan per trailer depends on the axle-weight limit. Fragile items go in timber crates, slabs on A-frames. Check whether powders and adhesives need dangerous-goods packing.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

How long does it take to Tehran Customs?

14 to 18 calendar days from the Yiwu or Shenzhen warehouse to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse, including exit clearance at Khorgos and inbound processing at Sarakhs.

Do you deliver door-to-door?

No. Delivery is to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse; the consignee's licensed broker clears the goods and arranges local delivery.

Is it charged by weight or by volume?

By chargeable weight &mdash; the greater of actual and volumetric. Stone and tiles are mostly heavy and bill on weight; the load plan per trailer depends on the axle-weight limit. Fragile items go in timber crates, slabs on A-frames.

What is the minimum for LCL?

1 CBM or 100 kg, at Yiwu and Shenzhen, on the weekly Tuesday and Friday consolidation to Tehran Customs.
