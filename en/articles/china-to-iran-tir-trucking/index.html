ROUTE

China to Iran by road: TIR trucking to Tehran Customs in 14–18 days

chinairantrucks runs sealed TIR trailers from consolidation hubs in China to the bonded customs yards of Tehran. This is first-hand linehaul — our own dispatch teams at Khorgos and in Tehran, no subcontracting layer — delivered warehouse-to-customs, not door-to-door. The consignee's licensed broker clears the cargo at destination.

  • 14–18 calendar days, origin hub to Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb)
  • Exit China at Khorgos (primary) or Irkeshtam; enter Iran at Sarakhs (primary) or Incheboron
  • Trade terms CPT / DAP Tehran Customs — the buyer's broker pays duty and collects
  • Weekly departures, Tuesday and Friday, from Yiwu and Shenzhen (Pinghu)
  • FTL and LCL consolidation from 1 CBM or 100 kg
  • CMR transit insurance, cover up to USD 250,000 per trailer load

What “hub-to-customs” means

We consolidate at Yiwu (Suxi), Shenzhen (Pinghu), Guangzhou (Baiyun) and Shanghai. Cargo can also be handed to us at the Khorgos supervised zone.

The trailer is sealed once, under a TIR carnet, and opened once — at the destination customs warehouse.

Delivery is to Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), Shahriyar Customs, or Aprin Dry Port. It is not to your address.

On arrival a warehouse entry receipt — Ghabz-e Anbar — is issued to your broker, who pays import duty in Iran's ASYCUDA system and releases the goods.

We do not perform Iran customs clearance. That is the consignee's licensed broker.

The route, stage by stage

Days 1–2 — Origin consolidation. The Yiwu and Shenzhen (Pinghu) warehouses receive the cargo, check the packing list against the invoice, palletise and load 13.6 m curtainsiders, and file the export pre-manifest.

Days 3–4 — Khorgos. The convoy clears Chinese export customs at the Khorgos Free Trade Zone. Customs verifies the load and applies the TIR seal plus a tamper-evident electronic lock.

Days 5–11 — Central Asia. Straight through Kazakhstan (Almaty–Shymkent) and Turkmenistan (Farap–Mary) under the TIR convention: no transit duty, no escrow bond, no unsealing, no transloading. Two drivers rotate.

Days 12–14 — Sarakhs. Entry into north-east Iran at the Sarakhs road crossing. Customs checks the seal, the shipment is recorded in Iran's ASYCUDA system, and a bonded in-country transit permit is issued to Tehran.

Days 15–18 — Tehran West Customs. The convoy delivers into the Gomrok Gharb bonded warehouse, or Aprin Dry Port. The goods are unloaded, the Ghabz-e Anbar is issued, and clearance passes to your broker.

The Irkeshtam crossing, via Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan, is the secondary routing. It carries a 0.4% invoice-value transit tax that the Khorgos–Kazakhstan corridor does not.

What we carry

General cargo, FTL and LCL: auto parts, electronics, machinery, building materials, consumer goods.

Class 9 lithium batteries — UN 3480 (cells and packs) and UN 3481 (batteries packed with equipment), plus industrial BESS and LiFePO4 packs. Trailers with fire-retardant partitions, to ADR standards. A complete 16-section MSDS and UN packaging documents are required.

Solar and power engineering: mono PV modules, industrial inverters, step-up transformers, on air-suspension trailers.

Out-of-gauge machinery: injection machines, CNC centres, presses, excavators, on 17.5 m step-frame low-beds.

Chemical materials and industrial resins with a valid MSDS.

Complete vehicles, EVs and turnkey plant projects are quoted separately.

Road versus sea and air

Road under TIR: 14–18 days, delivered into the Tehran customs bonded warehouse, sealed end to end, no transshipment.

Ocean via Bandar Abbas: 35–55 days to the container yard, then a separate inland leg to Tehran, with multiple port handlings.

Air to Tehran (IKA): 5–8 days, but severe limits on large single-cell batteries and out-of-gauge pieces, and the highest cost per kilo.

For batteries, chemicals and heavy machinery, road is usually the only mode that takes the cargo at a workable rate.

FAQ

Do you deliver door-to-door or DDP inside Iran?

No. We run cross-border linehaul only, on CPT / DAP terms, from China hubs to Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), Aprin Dry Port, or Shahriyar Customs. The consignee's licensed broker pays import duty and collects the goods.

How long is the transit to Tehran Customs?

14 to 18 calendar days from warehouse departure at Yiwu or Shenzhen to warehouse entry at Tehran West Customs, including exit clearance at Khorgos and inbound processing at Sarakhs.

Can you move standalone lithium-ion batteries (UN 3480)?

Yes. Under the TIR and ADR conventions, road freight can carry UN 3480 / 3481 Class 9 lithium batteries, energy storage systems and industrial raw materials, with a valid 16-section MSDS and UN packaging documentation.

What is the minimum volume for LCL?

One cubic metre (CBM) or 100 kg, at the Yiwu and Shenzhen (Pinghu) facilities, on the weekly Tuesday and Friday consolidation to Tehran Customs.

Which border crossings do you use?

Khorgos into Kazakhstan is the primary China exit; Irkeshtam via Kyrgyzstan is the secondary. Sarakhs is the primary Iran entry; Incheboron is the alternative.

Who pays Iranian import duty?

The consignee, through their licensed customs broker, in Iran's ASYCUDA system, after the goods reach the bonded warehouse and the Ghabz-e Anbar is issued.
