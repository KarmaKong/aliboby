How to request a China–Iran trucking quote

There are no published rates. Send three things and we return a rate on CPT / DAP Tehran Customs terms and the next scheduled departure.

  • Three items: origin hub; destination customs; cargo data (HS code, gross weight kg, volume CBM)
  • Lithium / chemical cargo: attach a 16-section MSDS and UN packaging details
  • Out-of-gauge machinery: attach a dimensioned weight drawing with lifting points
  • The reply gives a rate and the next Tuesday or Friday departure; clearance in Iran is the consignee's broker
  1. Origin hub — Yiwu, Shenzhen (Pinghu), or Guangzhou; or handover at the Khorgos supervised zone
  2. Destination customs — Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), Aprin Dry Port, or Mashhad Customs
  3. Cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, and volume in CBM

For special cargo

Class 9 lithium batteries and chemical cargo — attach a full 16-section MSDS and UN packaging details. Out-of-gauge machinery — attach a dimensioned weight drawing showing lifting points.

What you get back

A rate on CPT / DAP Tehran Customs terms and the next scheduled departure, Tuesday or Friday. Import duty and clearance in Iran are handled by the consignee's licensed broker.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Do you publish rates?

No. Freight is quoted per shipment because it moves with capacity, cargo class and season.

What if my cargo has lithium batteries?

Say so in the first line and attach the 16-section MSDS and UN packaging details. It is a core service, not an exception, but it must be declared up front.

How fast is the quote?

Usually same day once the three items are complete. Missing the destination customs or the volume is the usual delay.

Is customs clearance included in the quote?

No. The rate is to Tehran Customs on CPT / DAP terms; the consignee's broker clears and pays duty.
