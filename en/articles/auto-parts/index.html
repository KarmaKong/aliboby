AUTO

Shipping auto parts from China to Iran by truck

Auto parts from China to Iran by road. Consolidated at Yiwu, Shenzhen and Guangzhou, out through Khorgos on a sealed TIR trailer, delivered in 14–18 days to the bonded customs warehouse at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb). Hub-to-customs, not to the door, not by sea.

  • 14–18 calendar days, origin hub to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse
  • Hub-to-customs · CPT / DAP · the consignee's broker clears and pays duty
  • Route: Khorgos — Kazakhstan — Turkmenistan — Sarakhs — Tehran
  • FTL and LCL from 1 CBM or 100 kg, weekly Tuesday & Friday departures

The route

Origin hub → export clearance and sealing at Khorgos (TIR lock + electronic seal) → transit through Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan with no unsealing and no transloading → entry at Sarakhs → the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse, where a warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar) is issued to your broker. The Irkeshtam crossing via Kyrgyzstan is the secondary routing and carries a 0.4% invoice-value transit tax; Khorgos does not.

Describing the goods and loading

State the part number, the vehicle model and the material, and palletise and secure the load. Plastic and glass parts bill on volume; brake pads and bearings on weight. Assemblies with a small battery or an electronic control unit are declared as Class 9, with an MSDS.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

How long does it take to Tehran Customs?

14 to 18 calendar days from the Yiwu or Shenzhen warehouse to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse, including exit clearance at Khorgos and inbound processing at Sarakhs.

Do you deliver door-to-door?

No. Delivery is to the customs bonded warehouse; the consignee's licensed broker clears the goods and arranges local delivery.

Can parts with a small battery ship?

Yes, declared as Class 9 with an MSDS and UN packaging. Say so in the first line of the enquiry.

What is the minimum for LCL?

1 CBM or 100 kg, on the weekly Tuesday and Friday consolidation at Yiwu and Shenzhen.
