DAYS 22

22 days is the Tehran terminal

From Xinjiang, by truck, to Iran. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal.

  • 22 days counts to the Tehran terminal, not to your door
  • Terminal pickup and buyer's clearance are separate
  • Mashhad and Sarakhs are not given a day count — quote only
  • TIR does not make it faster

Where 22 days ends

It counts to the Tehran terminal, not to your premises. Pickup at the terminal and the buyer's customs clearance are on top of that.

Other cities

Mashhad and Sarakhs do not get the 22-day figure — they are quote only. Put the destination city in your enquiry; do not assume everything is 22 days.

What lengthens it

Queues at the crossing, truck changes, tight capacity in peak season, border inspection, incomplete documents.

Both corridors are about 22 days to the Tehran terminal: Wuqia (+0.4%) and Khorgos (no 0.4%).

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
