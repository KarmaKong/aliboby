DAYS

14–18 days is to Tehran Customs, not your door

The 14–18 day figure is the run from a consolidation hub in China to the bonded warehouse at Tehran West Customs. It is not door-to-door, and it is not DDP.

  • 14–18 days = China hub to the Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb) bonded warehouse
  • Warehouse handover and the buyer's clearance are separate steps on top
  • Mashhad Customs carries no day count — quote only
  • We do not quote 10–12 days; that is not this line's honest planning number

What the number covers

Departure from the Yiwu or Shenzhen hub, export clearance at Khorgos, transit through Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan under TIR, entry at Sarakhs, and delivery into the Gomrok Gharb bonded warehouse where the Ghabz-e Anbar is issued.

What it does not cover

Import duty payment, customs clearance in the ASYCUDA system, and delivery out of the warehouse to your premises. Those are handled by the consignee's licensed broker after the cargo arrives.

Why not 10 days

Some listings advertise 10–12 days. With a load sealed once at Khorgos, run under convoy scheduling across two transit countries, and passed through full customs steps at Sarakhs and Tehran, 14–18 days is the number we will hold to.

Other destinations

Tehran West Customs, Shahriyar and Aprin Dry Port take the 14–18 day window. Mashhad Customs is quoted case by case. State the destination customs in your enquiry.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Does 14–18 days include clearance and delivery in Iran?

No. It ends at the bonded warehouse. Duty, clearance and onward delivery are the consignee's broker.

Can it be faster than 14 days?

Occasionally a clean run lands at 14; we plan on 14–18. We do not promise 10–12.

Is delivery to my warehouse included?

No. Delivery is to the customs bonded warehouse. Collection from there is arranged by your broker after clearance.

What about Mashhad?

Mashhad Customs is not given a fixed day count and is quoted case by case.
