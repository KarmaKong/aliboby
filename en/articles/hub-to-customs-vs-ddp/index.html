HUB-TO-CUSTOMS

Hub-to-customs is not door delivery: China → Tehran West Customs CPT/DAP, not DDP

2026-09-07

China–Iran trucking on this line is hub-to-customs, not door-to-door. The terms are CPT / DAP Tehran Customs: cargo is delivered into the bonded warehouse at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb), and a warehouse entry receipt is issued. We do not clear Iran customs. We do not offer DDP. This page does not quote sea freight.

  • Hub-to-customs: CPT / DAP Tehran Customs; not door-to-door, not DDP
  • 14–18 calendar days cover origin hub → Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse only; Mashhad Customs is quote only, no day count
  • On arrival a warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar / قبض انبار) goes to the consignee’s broker; we do not do Iran clearance
  • Freight quoted on request. A quote needs: origin hub; destination customs; HS code · kg · CBM

Delivery point: hubs to Tehran West Customs

Origin consolidation hubs: Yiwu (Suxi), Shenzhen (Pinghu), Guangzhou (Baiyun), Shanghai. Cargo can also be handed over at the Khorgos supervised zone (CY / CFS).

Weekly departures, Tuesday and Friday, from the Yiwu and Shenzhen (Pinghu) facilities. LCL from 1 CBM or 100 kg.

The named place is the bonded warehouse at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb). Aprin Dry Port and Shahriyar Customs are also destination customs. The contract says CPT / DAP Tehran Customs. The truck stops at customs, not at the buyer’s door.

Ghabz-e Anbar handoff

When the cargo enters the bonded warehouse, a warehouse entry receipt is issued — Ghabz-e Anbar / قبض انبار. The receipt goes to the consignee’s licensed broker.

The broker clears in Iran’s ASYCUDA system, pays import duty, and arranges collection. We do not do Iran customs clearance.

Appoint the broker before the truck arrives. Once the receipt is issued it must go to the broker at once, or the cargo sits in the warehouse. How to choose a broker: Choosing your Iran customs broker.

What the 14–18 days cover, and what TIR does

14–18 calendar days: origin hub → Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse. That count does not include Iran clearance or city delivery. Mashhad Customs has no published day count; quote only.

TIR is a customs seal at origin plus one carnet. Across Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan that means fewer opens and transfers — fewer, not none. TIR does not by itself cut the freight rate or the transit time. See How TIR works on the China–Iran corridor and China to Iran road transit time: 14–18 days to Tehran Customs.

The CKU railway (China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan) is under construction and not open. Iran cargo on this line is still road. This page does not quote sea or rail.

Exit Khorgos primary, entry Sarakhs

Primary corridor: exit at Khorgos, then Kazakhstan (Almaty–Shymkent), Turkmenistan (Farap–Mary), entry at Sarakhs, then Tehran West Customs.

Irkeshtam is the secondary exit, via Kyrgyzstan / Uzbekistan. Sarakhs is the primary Iran entry; Incheboron is secondary.

The 0.4% is an invoice-value tax, not freight, and it applies only on the Irkeshtam secondary routing. The Khorgos–Kazakhstan corridor does not add it. See Khorgos or Irkeshtam.

FAQ

Do you offer DDP or door-to-door delivery?

No. This line is hub-to-customs: CPT / DAP Tehran Customs, into the bonded warehouse. Iran clearance, duty, and inland delivery are the consignee's licensed broker.

Where does CPT / DAP delivery end?

At the bonded warehouse at Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb). Aprin Dry Port and Shahriyar Customs are also destination customs. Not the buyer's warehouse door.

What is the Ghabz-e Anbar, and who receives it?

The warehouse entry receipt issued when the cargo enters the bonded warehouse. It goes to the consignee's broker, who clears in ASYCUDA.

Do the 14–18 days include Iran clearance or city delivery?

No. The 14–18 calendar days cover origin hub to the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse only. Mashhad Customs has no published day count; quote only.

Is the 0.4% freight, and does Khorgos add it?

It is not freight. The 0.4% is an invoice-value tax on the Irkeshtam (Kyrgyzstan) secondary routing only. The primary Khorgos–Kazakhstan corridor does not add it.
