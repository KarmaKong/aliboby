Documents checklist

On China–Iran trucking, missing documents hold cargo at the border and lengthen the schedule. The usual list is below; the carrier and destination-country requirements are the final word.

  • Basics: commercial invoice, packing list, contract — item, quantity and value must match the goods
  • Certificate of origin: by destination country and trade term (CO / Form)
  • TIR moves: the convoy carries a carnet, sealed at origin
  • Dangerous / lithium / dual-use / encryption goods: MSDS, assessment or permit — state it in the first line

Base documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, contract. Item name, quantity, and value must match the actual goods.

Certificate of origin

Issued by destination country and trade term; commonly a CO or Form.

TIR carnet

A TIR convoy carries the carnet and is sealed at origin. See TIR.

Special cargo

Dangerous goods, lithium batteries, dual-use items, encryption equipment — MSDS, assessment, or permit. State it in the first line, not at loading. See electronics (Chinese).

Destination clearance

Clearance at the Tehran terminal is on the buyer; the clearance agent and paperwork are arranged separately.

Send the four enquiry items first; documents follow the checklist once the rate is set. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal, not the door.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
