COST

What the freight depends on

We do not publish rates. These are the things that set the freight.

  • No published rates
  • Chargeable weight: the greater of actual weight or volume
  • The crossing changes the price; Wuqia adds a separate 0.4% (a tax, not freight)
  • Peak season, border queues, and cargo type (dangerous / permit) all move it

Chargeable weight

The greater of actual weight or volume. Light bulky cargo prices on volume; dense cargo on weight.

The crossing

Wuqia (via Kyrgyzstan) and Khorgos (via Kazakhstan) price differently. Wuqia also adds a 0.4% invoice-value tax — a tax, not freight, billed separately. Khorgos does not.

Destination city

Mashhad, Tehran, Sarakhs. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal; other cities are priced separately.

FTL or LTL

A full truck is one price; part load is shared per consignment. See FTL or LTL.

Season and border

Queues, truck changes, and tight capacity in peak season all affect price and schedule.

The cargo itself

Dangerous, dual-use, or permit-controlled goods change whether it can move and what it costs. Put it in the first line.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
