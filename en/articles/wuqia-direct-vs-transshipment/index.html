Wuqia to Iran: direct truck or transshipment

2026-10-08

The Wuqia corridor is sold as two movements, not one number. Both run Xinjiang → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan → Iran, and finish at the destination yard. The difference is how often the cargo is transshipped.

  • Direct: Wuqia → Bukhara (one transshipment) → Iran
  • Transshipment: Wuqia → Osh (transshipment) → Bukhara (transshipment) → Iran; or at the Iranian border
  • Warehouse / yard to destination yard; no door-to-door, no DDP
  • The buyer clears in Iran at Sarakhs or Lotfabad

One corridor, two movements

Equipment is a tilt / curtain-side trailer, not a container. TIR means a seal at origin plus one carnet. Fewer openings and truck changes — not none. It does not change transit time or freight.

FAQ

What is the difference between direct and transshipment?

Direct: Wuqia → Bukhara (one transshipment) → Iran. Transshipment: Wuqia → Osh → Bukhara → Iran, or transshipment at the Iranian border.

Do the published days apply to Khorgos?

No. The table is Wuqia only, counted from the border. We do not publish days for Khorgos.

Does TIR change transit time or freight?

No. TIR means fewer openings and truck changes — not none. It does not change time or freight.

Do you offer door-to-door or DDP?

No. Warehouse / yard to destination yard. The buyer clears in Iran at Sarakhs or Lotfabad.

Are rates on the page?
