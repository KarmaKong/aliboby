Why this lane is FTL-only

2026-09-04

Most China–Iran road freight pages sell two products: a full trailer and a share of one. This one sells one. Below is the reasoning, the trailer limit, and what to do if you genuinely only have a few cubic metres.

  • FTL only — one shipper per trailer, sealed at the exit gateway
  • Up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes per trailer, tilt (curtain-side) equipment
  • Wuqia to Tehran currently 21–23 days direct; Khorgos on request
  • No published rates; CPT/DAP to the customs yard

The two products, and why we kept one

Groupage (LCL) splits a trailer between shippers and bills by cubic metre. It is cheaper per CBM for small loads and it is the right product for a lot of corridors. On this one it is not: the corridor runs through two border crossings and a transload, and every additional shipper on a trailer adds a document set that can stop the whole load.

A full trailer carries one declaration, one seal and one consignee. When something is wrong with the paperwork, it is your paperwork and your broker can fix it in hours instead of waiting for a stranger's corrective declaration.

What a full trailer carries

A tilt trailer takes up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes. Heavy machinery, lithium under UN 3480/3481, solar modules, furniture and building materials all travel as full loads; a 13.6 m curtainsider or a 17.5 m low-bed is chosen per cargo, not per pallet count.

What changes if you need LCL

You need an operator that actually consolidates on this corridor, and you need to accept the shared-trailer risk: someone else's vague description can hold your pallet at the border. The alternative is to build volume upstream and ship when you have a trailer — slower to start, cleaner to clear.

What the quote needs

Origin hub, destination customs and cargo data: HS code, gross kg, CBM. Rates come back by email with a validity date; transit ranges are current, not guaranteed, and are counted from the Wuqia crossing.

FAQ

Do you do LTL or LCL?

No. This lane is FTL only; groupage and trailer shares are not offered.

What is the trailer limit?

Up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes per trailer, on a tilt trailer.

Why not offer both?

On this corridor a shared trailer adds document sets that can stop the whole load at the border; we would rather run one shipper per trailer.

How do I get a price?

Send origin hub, destination customs, HS code, gross kg and CBM. Rates come by email with a validity date.
