Road transit time: 22 days is the Tehran terminal

From Xinjiang, by truck, to Iran. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal.

  • About 22 days to the Tehran terminal
  • Counts to the terminal, not the door; pickup and clearance are separate
  • Lengtheners: crossing queues, truck changes, peak season, inspection, incomplete documents
  • Mashhad and Sarakhs are quote only

Where the count ends

It ends at the Tehran terminal, not your premises. Terminal pickup and the buyer's clearance are on top.

Other cities

Mashhad and Sarakhs do not carry the 22-day figure — quote only. State the destination city in your enquiry.

What lengthens it

Queues at the crossing, truck changes, tight capacity in peak season, border inspection, incomplete documents.

TIR is not faster

TIR means fewer openings and truck changes. It does not fix a day count. See TIR.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
