DAYS

Road transit time: 14–18 days to Tehran Customs

From a consolidation hub in China, by sealed TIR truck, to the bonded customs warehouse at Tehran West Customs. 14 to 18 calendar days, warehouse to warehouse.

  • 14–18 calendar days, origin hub to the Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb) bonded warehouse
  • Rough split: 4 days origin + Khorgos, 7 days Kazakhstan + Turkmenistan, 3–5 days Sarakhs to Tehran
  • The count ends at the bonded warehouse, not your address; duty and clearance are on top
  • Mashhad Customs carries no day count — quote only
  • Lengtheners: border queues, peak-season capacity, inspection, incomplete documents, Kazakhstan winter roads

The breakdown

Days 1–2. Consolidation at the Yiwu or Shenzhen (Pinghu) warehouse; packing list checked against the invoice; loading and export pre-manifest.

Days 3–4. Chinese export clearance at the Khorgos Free Trade Zone; the TIR seal and an electronic lock are applied.

Days 5–11. Kazakhstan (Almaty–Shymkent) then Turkmenistan (Farap–Mary) under the TIR carnet; no unsealing, no transloading; two drivers rotate.

Days 12–14. Entry at the Sarakhs crossing; seal check; the shipment is recorded in Iran's ASYCUDA system; bonded transit permit to Tehran.

Days 15–18. Delivery into the Gomrok Gharb bonded warehouse; unloading; the warehouse entry receipt (Ghabz-e Anbar) is issued to your broker.

Where the count ends

It ends when the cargo is in the bonded warehouse and the Ghabz-e Anbar is issued. Import duty, customs clearance and delivery from the warehouse are the consignee's licensed broker.

What lengthens it

Queues at Khorgos or Sarakhs, tight capacity in peak season, physical inspection, incomplete or inconsistent documents, and winter road conditions across Kazakhstan.

TIR is not a speed setting

A TIR carnet means one seal and fewer openings. It does not shorten the day count and it does not set the freight. See how TIR works.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

How many days is China to Tehran Customs by road?

14 to 18 calendar days from warehouse departure at Yiwu or Shenzhen to warehouse entry at Tehran West Customs, including exit clearance at Khorgos and inbound processing at Sarakhs.

Does the 14–18 days include customs clearance in Iran?

No. The count ends at the bonded warehouse. Import duty payment, clearance and local delivery are handled by the consignee's licensed broker afterwards.

What about Mashhad?

Mashhad Customs is not given a day count. State the destination customs in your enquiry and it is quoted case by case.

Does TIR make it faster?

No. TIR reduces openings and removes per-border deposits, but the transit time is set by distance, border throughput and convoy scheduling, not by the carnet.
