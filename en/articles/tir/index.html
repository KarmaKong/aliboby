TIR

How TIR works on the China–Iran corridor

A TIR carnet lets a sealed truck cross Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan without a customs deposit at each border and without being opened on the way. The load is sealed once, at Khorgos, and opened once, at the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse.

  • One carnet covers the whole transit from Khorgos to Tehran West Customs
  • Sealed by Chinese customs at Khorgos; opened by Iranian customs at the bonded warehouse
  • No per-border security deposit, no unsealing, no transloading in transit
  • TIR does not shorten the 14–18 day transit and does not set the freight
  • We are a registered TIR international road-transport operator

What the carnet does

It removes the security deposit customs would otherwise require at each transit border, and the seal means customs in Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan do not strip the load to inspect it.

Where it is sealed and opened

Chinese customs apply the TIR seal and a tamper-evident electronic lock at the Khorgos Free Trade Zone. The seal is broken at the Gomrok Gharb bonded warehouse, under Iranian customs, when the Ghabz-e Anbar is issued.

What TIR is not

It is not a speed setting — 14–18 days to Tehran West Customs still stands. It is not a freight rate. It is not door-to-door. The 10–12 days some listings advertise is not this line.

No published rates. To get a quote, send three things: the origin hub; the destination customs (Tehran West Customs / Aprin / Mashhad); and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

FAQ

Does TIR make the shipment faster?

No. TIR removes per-border deposits and keeps the load sealed, but transit time is set by distance, border throughput and convoy scheduling. Plan on 14–18 days to Tehran West Customs.

Who holds the TIR carnet?

The carrier. We are a registered TIR operator and the convoy carries the carnet; the shipper does not need to arrange one.

Is the truck opened during transit?

No. Under the carnet the load is sealed at Khorgos and stays sealed across Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan until the Tehran West Customs bonded warehouse.

Does TIR cover customs clearance in Iran?

No. TIR is a transit instrument. Import clearance and duty at Tehran Customs are handled by the consignee's licensed broker.
