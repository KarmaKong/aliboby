TIR

TIR on the China–Iran route

TIR means a customs seal applied at origin and a single carnet covering the whole transit. Fewer openings along the way, fewer truck changes. Fewer, not zero.

  • TIR = customs seal at origin + one carnet for the whole transit
  • Fewer openings and truck changes en route; fewer, not zero
  • TIR does not fix the transit time or the freight
  • Both corridors can run under TIR: Wuqia and Khorgos

TIR does not set a day count

TIR does not fix 22 days, and it does not fix the freight. Fewer openings is not the same as faster. The 10 or 12 days you may see elsewhere are not this route. Freight is quoted on request.

Two corridors

Wuqia and Khorgos. About 22 days to the Tehran terminal, which is the terminal, not your door. Wuqia adds a 0.4% invoice-value tax. Khorgos does not.

Cargo: auto parts, electronics, machinery, building materials.

To get a quote, send four things: item name; quantity or weight; destination city; Wuqia or Khorgos.
