We do not offer LCL on this lane — FTL only

2026-09-04

chinairantrucks does not consolidate cargo. On this lane we move full trailers (FTL) from a China loading hub to the destination customs yard in Iran, under CPT or DAP. There is no LCL product, no trailer share and no weekly groupage departure.

  • FTL only — no LCL, no consolidation, no trailer share
  • One trailer carries up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes on a tilt (curtain-side) trailer
  • Wuqia to Tehran currently 21–23 days direct; Khorgos transit time quoted on request
  • CPT/DAP to the customs yard — no DDP, no door delivery, no Iran-side clearance

What “no LCL” means in practice

A trailer belongs to one shipper. It is loaded at the origin hub, sealed at the exit gateway and cleared as one consignment. Nobody waits for a co-loader to fill the remaining cubic metres, and no other party's documents can hold your cargo at the border.

That is the whole reason this lane is FTL-only. Groupage works when volumes are steady and the co-loaders are disciplined; on a China–Iran road corridor, one weak declaration on a shared trailer costs every shipper on it days.

Three things that stop groupage cargo at the border

These are the mistakes we see most often in enquiries that were sized for a shared trailer. They are worth knowing even if you end up shipping full trailers.

1. Volume and weight declared low

Charges are built on the greater of actual and volumetric weight. Rounding down invites a reweigh and a re-cube at the gateway, and on a consolidated trailer it can push the whole load over the axle limit.

2. A vague cargo description

“Assorted goods” is not a description a customs officer can clear. State the item and the HS code; on a shared trailer a vague line holds everyone, not just your pallet.

3. Undeclared battery or hazardous content

Lithium is offloaded at the border when it is not declared up front. Compliant lithium moves with us as a full trailer, with a 16-section MSDS and UN packaging documents in the first line of the enquiry.

If your load is small

Two honest options: build the volume upstream with your own forwarder until you have a full trailer, or place the cargo with a groupage operator that actually runs consolidation on this corridor. We do not sell trailer shares, so a 3 CBM enquiry will get a polite no, not a padded quote.

What an FTL enquiry needs

Three inputs: the origin hub (Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai), the destination customs (Tehran West Customs, Aprin or Mashhad), and the cargo data — HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM. Rates are not published; they come back by email with a validity date.

Transit figures quoted here are current operating ranges from the Wuqia crossing, not a guaranteed transit, and they exclude the China-side leg to the border.

FAQ

Do you offer LCL or consolidation?

No. This lane moves full trailers only (FTL), hub to destination customs under CPT/DAP. For a quote send: origin hub, destination customs, HS code, gross kg, CBM.

What is the minimum volume?

There is no minimum volume, because there is no LCL. You book a trailer, up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes.

My cargo is only 3 CBM. Can you help?

Not on this lane. We do not sell trailer shares; a small load needs a groupage operator, or it waits until it fills a trailer.

Can lithium batteries move with you?

Yes, as a full trailer, declared up front with a 16-section MSDS and UN packaging documents. We do not consolidate lithium with other shippers' cargo.
