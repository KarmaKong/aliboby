What the Bukhara transload means on a Wuqia direct truck

2026-10-19

On chinairantrucks, “direct truck” does not mean the trailer is never opened. The Wuqia direct routing is Wuqia → Bukhara (one transload) → Iran. Direct or transshipment sets out the two movements; this page is that one stop at Bukhara.

  • Direct = one transload at Bukhara, not an unbroken seal to the buyer’s warehouse
  • TIR means fewer unsealings, not none; it does not change transit time or rate
  • Sellable destinations: Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs (Aprin). Entry via Sarakhs
  • Days counted from Wuqia: currently 21–23 to Tehran, 18–21 to Mashhad on a direct truck — current range, not a guarantee

Direct means one stop, not zero

If you hear “direct”, count Bukhara. The load leaves one tractor unit and goes onto the next. A transshipment truck transloads at Osh and again at Bukhara, or at the Iranian border. Fewer transloads is not zero transloads.

What TIR does at Bukhara

TIR is a customs seal at origin plus one carnet. That means fewer opens in the transit countries. It does not cancel the Bukhara transload, and it does not change transit time or the freight rate.

Where the truck hands over after Bukhara

Sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs (Gomrok Gharb / Aprin). Entry is via Sarakhs. Iran clearance is the buyer’s broker’s job. Terms are CPT / DAP, hub to destination customs. Not door-to-door, not DDP. The unit is a curtainsider / tilt trailer, not a container.

Days start at the Wuqia crossing

Not from Yiwu or Shenzhen. Direct trucks currently run 21–23 days to Tehran and 18–21 days to Mashhad. That is the line as it is running now, not a guarantee. The China hub-to-Wuqia leg is stated separately, typically within about 7 days — an estimate, not added into a single headline figure. No Khorgos day count.

FAQ

Does direct mean zero transload?

No. Wuqia → Bukhara (one transload) → Iran.

Does TIR keep the seal closed at Bukhara?

It reduces opens. It does not eliminate them. TIR does not change transit time or the freight rate.

Door delivery?

No. Destination customs yard only. CPT / DAP. Not DDP. The buyer clears in Iran.
