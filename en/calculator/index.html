How to measure a line

A few hundred cartons of the same size are one line. Measure one unit in centimetres, multiply length × width × height, divide by 1,000,000 to get CBM, then multiply by the quantity. Weigh one unit and multiply by the same quantity for gross weight. Goods on a pallet are measured with the pallet: its footprint and its full height are part of the unit.

What one trailer takes

Up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes. Whichever limit is reached first decides: light bulky cargo runs out of volume, dense cargo runs out of weight. Volume is usable only as far as shape and stacking allow — a machine base too tall to stack wastes the space above it.

If the total goes over

A result above one trailer means two trailers or more. Anything over-length or over-width is checked and priced per shipment; this calculator only compares volume and weight with the trailer limit.

FAQ

Is CBM the same as cubic metres?

Yes. One CBM is one cubic metre: a box 100 × 100 × 100 cm, or 1,000,000 cm³.

Can I book part of a trailer?

No. This lane is FTL only — we do not consolidate cargo from several shippers, so a part-trailer booking is not offered here.

Should a pallet be its own line?

A pallet is measured with the goods on it: outer dimensions and total height, pallet included.

What if my cargo is heavy but small?

The 22-tonne limit is per trailer. Dense cargo reaches it before the volume limit, so the result may say two trailers for a load that looks small.

Is the result a booking confirmation?

No. It is a planning check against the published trailer limit; final loading and the dispatcher's check happen at the warehouse.
